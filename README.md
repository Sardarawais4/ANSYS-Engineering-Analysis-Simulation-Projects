ANSYS Engineering Analysis & Simulation Projects
Overview

This repository contains a collection of engineering analysis and simulation projects performed using ANSYS Workbench and related ANSYS tools. The projects cover different areas of mechanical engineering, including thermal, structural, fluid flow, CFD, aerodynamic, and heat transfer analyses.

The work demonstrates practical experience in preparing geometry, generating meshes, applying engineering boundary conditions and loads, solving simulation models, and interpreting numerical results.

Purpose

The purpose of these projects is to develop practical skills in Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) and numerical simulation and to apply engineering principles to real-world mechanical engineering problems.

These projects are part of my mechanical engineering learning and demonstrate my practical experience with ANSYS-based engineering simulation and analysis.
